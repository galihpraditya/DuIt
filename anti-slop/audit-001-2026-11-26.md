## HIGH — Hard Gates & Structural Hierarchy Blockers

1. **[HIGH | R-25] Low-contrast `text-slate-400` labels fail WCAG AA contrast (`2.56:1` on `#ffffff`)**
   - **Location:** `src/App.tsx:1047,1093-1127`, `src/components/analytics/KpiCards.tsx:121`, `src/components/settings/SettingsView.tsx:237`, `src/components/transactions/TransactionModal.tsx:415,525,538,557,626`
   - **Reason:** Secondary metrics, email subtext, and form uppercase labels render in `#94a3b8` against `#ffffff`, falling well below the `4.5:1` WCAG AA normal-text requirement (`#64748b` `text-slate-500` or `#475569` `text-slate-600` is required).

2. **[HIGH | R-39] Clickable `<div onClick>` containers wrap nested `<button>` actions**
   - **Location:** `src/components/categories/CategoryManager.tsx:333-358`, `src/components/budget/BudgetManager.tsx:223-231`
   - **Reason:** Non-semantic `<div>` click handlers with nested action buttons prevent keyboard (`Tab`/`Enter`/`Space`) navigation and fail to expose interactive semantics to screen readers.

3. **[HIGH | R-03] Sub-44px mobile touch targets & cramped icon controls**
   - **Location:** `src/App.tsx:974,1003,1032` (`p-1.5` month chevrons & nominal visibility button), `src/components/categories/CategoryManager.tsx:358-410`, `src/components/transactions/TransactionList.tsx`
   - **Reason:** Critical navigation and row/card action buttons render under `44x44px` on touch screens without sufficient spacing, increasing mis-taps on mobile viewports.

4. **[HIGH | R-05 / C-3] Analytics stacks 5–6 heavy blocks and duplicates `Total Pengeluaran`**
   - **Location:** `src/components/analytics/AnalyticsView.tsx:280-338`, `src/components/analytics/CashFlowCard.tsx:57-82`, `src/components/analytics/KpiCards.tsx:35-43`
   - **Reason:** In `Arus Kas` mode, `CashFlowCard`, `SafeToSpendCard`, 4 `KpiCards`, 2 charts, and `CategoryRankingTable` stack vertically while `Total Pengeluaran` is repeated across both `CashFlowCard` and `KpiCards`.

5. **[HIGH | C-3] `SafeToSpendCard` forces a tall empty-state CTA card when no budgets exist**
   - **Location:** `src/components/analytics/SafeToSpendCard.tsx:22-75`
   - **Reason:** When the user has not configured category budgets, a full dashed box sits permanently above charts, adding vertical bloat with zero analytical data.

6. **[HIGH | R-05 / C-3] Transactions tab stacks 3 separate cards before showing the first transaction**
   - **Location:** `src/App.tsx:932-1127`, `src/components/transactions/QuickPresets.tsx`, `src/components/transactions/TransactionList.tsx:300-380`
   - **Reason:** A 5-metric Hero card (plus progress bar and month selector), `QuickPresets` strip, and multi-select Search/Filter card consume mobile above-the-fold height before any transaction row appears.

7. **[HIGH | R-14 / C-3] `BudgetManager` renders full `.glass-card` blocks for every unbudgeted category**
   - **Location:** `src/components/budget/BudgetManager.tsx:220-360`
   - **Reason:** Unbudgeted categories render identical large cards (`"Belum ada batas anggaran" + "+ Pasang Limit"`), inflating page length and burying active budgets instead of grouping unbudgeted items into a compact add strip.

8. **[HIGH | R-05 / C-3] `SettingsView` embeds all 6 sections of `ThemeCustomizer` inline across 8 cards**
   - **Location:** `src/components/settings/SettingsView.tsx:212-744`, `src/components/settings/ThemeCustomizer.tsx:1-482`
   - **Reason:** Expanding mode, 3 surface palettes, 7 accent swatches + custom picker, radius, and 5 font cards inline inside Settings pushes daily controls (`Pendapatan`, `Pengingat`, `AI`, `Excel`) far below the fold.

## MEDIUM — Purpose-Gate, Card Uniformity & Visual Restraint

9. **[MEDIUM | R-14 / R-11] Uniform `.glass-card rounded-3xl` boxes applied indiscriminately to every section**
   - **Location:** `src/App.tsx`, `src/components/analytics/AnalyticsView.tsx`, `src/components/categories/CategoryManager.tsx`, `src/components/settings/SettingsView.tsx`
   - **Reason:** Wrapping headers, filters, rows, and nested settings groups in identical shadowed `rounded-3xl` shells creates visual fatigue and obscures hierarchy between summary surfaces and list containers.

10. **[MEDIUM | C-3] `KpiCards` renders `"Frekuensi Belanja"` when viewing `Pendapatan`**
    - **Location:** `src/components/analytics/KpiCards.tsx:115`, `src/constants/translations.ts:620,1082`
    - **Reason:** Hardcoding `t.kpiFrequency` without an income variant mislabels income count as spending frequency (or can be removed/consolidated with top-level summary count).

11. **[MEDIUM | R-05 / C-3] `CategoryManager` duplicates counts across header & tabs and prints `"Tanpa limit"` filler**
    - **Location:** `src/components/categories/CategoryManager.tsx:260-305,450`
    - **Reason:** Category count appears in both the header badge and type tabs, while unbudgeted cards display `"Tanpa limit"` placeholder copy rather than omitting empty budget lines cleanly.

12. **[MEDIUM | R-32] Missing consistent keyboard `:focus-visible` indicator across controls**
    - **Location:** `src/index.css`, `src/App.tsx:974-1035`, `src/components/analytics/AnalyticsView.tsx:210-255`, `src/components/transactions/TransactionList.tsx`
    - **Reason:** Interactive buttons, tabs, and selects lack unified high-contrast `:focus-visible` rings, reducing keyboard usability on desktop.
